---

Last night I didn't go out for Halloween. I spent the weekend in San Francisco, mostly indoors. I've been here for a week doing business, and the plan was to get back to Mexico City on Friday in time for Día de Muertos. That didn't happen.

My friend Austin Diamond (amazing name, yes, I know) kindly offered to let me stay at his place.

On Saturday night I stayed home working and got inspired to read a book from his library. One thing led to another. I really admire his sense of style, and I thought it'd be a great idea to remake his living room in 3D and make his entire library browsable and readable.

So I took a couple of photos. He also has an incredible view. Then I just started making it.

I left him a little letter inside it:

> To my distinguished friend Austin Diamond: You've inspired me to read some of the books in your library, so I thought I'd give you a gift of my own and digitalize your entire library, indexed by category, and so on.
>
> \- Luc Succes

And here's the fake one. All 194 books are on the shelves, in the order he keeps them, with spines cut from my photos. Click a shelf to move closer, then click a book and it opens.
